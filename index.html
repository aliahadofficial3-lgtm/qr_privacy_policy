Fast, secure, offline-first QR scanner & custom QR code generator

Our Core Privacy Principle

Your data belongs strictly to you. QR Toolkit is architected as an offline-first utility. We do not require accounts, we do not operate telemetry or user-tracking servers, and we never transmit your scanned or generated QR code content across the network.

1 Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Toolkit ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you download, install, or use our mobile application on Android devices.

By using QR Toolkit, you consent to the data practices described in this document. If you do not agree with any terms of this policy, please refrain from using the application.

2 Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many online services, QR Toolkit does not collect, record, or transmit personal data to our own servers. Specifically:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email, phone number, or name to use the app.
  • No Content Transmission: The text, URLs, contact vCards, Wi-Fi credentials, or messages contained inside QR codes you scan or generate are processed strictly on your device.
  • No First-Party Analytics or Tracking: We do not deploy proprietary tracking beacons, keystroke logging, or behavioral tracking algorithms.

3 Device Permissions & How They Are Used

QR Toolkit only requests Android permissions strictly required to provide scanning and offline functionality. Here is full transparency regarding each permission:

Permission Type Purpose & Data Handling
android.permission.CAMERA Runtime Permission Required solely to display the live camera viewfinder and detect QR codes/barcodes in real time using Google ML Kit on-device vision. Camera frames are analyzed locally in volatile memory and are immediately discarded. No video or photos are saved or uploaded.
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal Permission Used solely to trigger physical haptic feedback upon successful code detection. You can toggle this setting on or off in the Settings menu at any time.
Photo Picker (Zero-Permission) System Capability When you choose to scan a barcode from an image or embed a custom logo into a generated QR code, the app utilizes the native Android Photo Picker. The app only receives access to the specific image file selected by you, and processes it in local RAM.
android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Permissions Used by third-party advertising SDKs (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob) to load advertising banners and interstitials, and to verify network availability when you voluntarily tap an external URL.

How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising

You have direct control over advertising personalization on your Android device:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID or toggle Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Once opted out, you will still see ads, but they will be contextual rather than personalized based on your activity across other apps.

5 Local Storage & User Data Control

QR Toolkit uses an isolated, on-device SQLite database (Android Room) to store:

  • Your scan history (if enabled in Settings).
  • Your saved / bookmarked favorite QR codes.
  • Your application preferences (dark/light theme, haptic feedback, sound effects).

You retain total control over this data:

  • Clear History at Any Time: Open Settings → History & Storage → Clear All History to permanently purge all scan and creation records from the device database.
  • Toggle History Saving: You can completely disable saving scan history in the Settings menu.
  • Uninstalling the App: Deleting QR Toolkit from your Android device immediately and permanently deletes all sandboxed files and database records.

6 Security & Safe Scanning

We take security seriously:

  • Safe Link Previews: By default, QR Toolkit does not automatically open scanned web links. You can safely review the destination URL before choosing to visit it in your browser.
  • Isolated App Sandbox: All data is stored within Android's sandboxed storage, inaccessible to other standard applications on your device.
  • Local Processing: Machine learning inference for barcode detection is performed on-device by Google ML Kit, eliminating network interception risks.

7 Children's Privacy

QR Toolkit is a general-purpose utility and is not directed towards children under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

If a parent or guardian becomes aware that a child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through the app, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate measures.
